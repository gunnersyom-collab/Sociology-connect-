🧭 Research Process

  1. Choose a research topic – Identify a clear social issue.
  2. Review literature – Read previous studies related to your topic.
  3. Develop research questions – Create questions your study will answer.
  4. Choose methodology – Select qualitative, quantitative or mixed methods.
  5. Collect data – Gather information ethically and systematically.
  6. Analyze data – Organize and interpret your findings.
  7. Write the report – Present your findings, discussion and conclusion.
  8. Reference your sources – Give proper credit to academic sources.

❓ Research FAQ

What is a research problem?

A research problem is a specific social issue or question that a researcher wants to investigate.

What is a research proposal?

A research proposal explains what you want to study, why the study is important and how you plan to conduct it.

What is qualitative research?

Qualitative research explores people's experiences, meanings, opinions and social realities.

What is quantitative research?

Quantitative research uses numerical data and statistical methods to study relationships, patterns or differences.
