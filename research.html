🧭 Research Process

  1. Choose a Topic – Select a clear social issue.
  2. Identify the Problem – Explain the problem.
  3. Review Literature – Read previous studies.
  4. Develop Questions – Create research questions.
  5. Set Objectives – Define objectives.
  6. Choose Methodology – Select research methods.
  7. Collect Data – Gather information ethically.
  8. Analyze Data – Interpret findings.
  9. Write Report – Present results clearly.
  10. Reference Sources – Give proper academic credit.

❓ Research FAQ

What is research?

Research is a systematic process of collecting and analyzing information.

Why is research important?

It helps solve social problems through evidence.

What is qualitative research?

It explores experiences, meanings and opinions.

What is quantitative research?

It uses numerical data and statistical methods.

What is a literature review?

It examines previous academic studies on a topic.
