⚖️ Qualitative vs Quantitative Research

🗣️ Qualitative

Explores experiences, opinions and meanings.

  • Interviews
  • Focus Groups
  • Observation
  • Thematic Analysis

📈 Quantitative

Uses numbers and measurable data.

  • Surveys
  • Questionnaires
  • Statistics
  • Charts & Tables

❓ Research FAQ

What is research?

Research is a systematic process of collecting, analyzing and interpreting information to answer a question.

Why is research important?

Research helps solve social problems and supports better decision-making.

What is a research problem?

A research problem is an issue that requires systematic investigation.

What is a research objective?

It explains what the researcher wants to achieve.

What is research methodology?

It explains how data will be collected and analyzed.

What is a literature review?

It examines previous academic studies related to the topic.

What is qualitative research?

It explores experiences, meanings and opinions.

What is quantitative research?

It uses numerical data and statistical methods.
