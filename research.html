🔬 Research Process

1. Choose a Topic

Select a clear and researchable issue.

2. Identify the Problem

Explain the problem that needs investigation.

3. Review Literature

Study previous research and academic sources.

4. Develop Questions

Create questions that guide your research.

5. Choose Methodology

Select suitable research methods.

6. Collect Data

Gather information from appropriate participants or sources.

7. Analyze Data

Analyze and interpret the collected information.

8. Write the Report

Present findings, conclusions and recommendations.

📊 Qualitative vs Quantitative Research

Qualitative Quantitative
Focuses on meanings and experiences. Focuses on numerical data.
Uses interviews, observations and focus groups. Uses surveys, measurements and statistical data.
Usually uses smaller samples. Can use larger samples.
Produces descriptive findings. Produces numerical findings.

📚 APA 7 Quick Guide

Book:
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.


Journal Article:
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, volume(issue), pages.


Website:
Author/Organization. (Year). Title of page. URL

📋 Data Collection Methods

Interview

Collect information through direct questions.

Questionnaire

Collect structured responses from participants.

Observation

Collect information by observing behaviors or situations.

Focus Group

Collect information through guided group discussion.

Document Review

Use existing documents and records as sources of data.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a research topic?

A research topic is the specific issue or subject that a researcher wants to investigate.

What is a research question?

A research question is a clear question that guides the investigation.

What is a research objective?

A research objective explains what the study intends to achieve.

What is an abstract?

An abstract is a short summary of the main parts of a research study.

What is APA 7?

APA 7 is a style used for academic writing, citations and references.
