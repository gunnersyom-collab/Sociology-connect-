📊 Qualitative vs Quantitative Research

🗣️ Qualitative Research

  • Focuses on meanings and experiences.
  • Uses words and detailed descriptions.
  • Often explores social experiences.
  • Common methods include interviews and focus groups.

📈 Quantitative Research

  • Focuses on measurable variables.
  • Uses numerical data.
  • Often examines patterns and relationships.
  • May use questionnaires and statistical analysis.

📖 APA 7 Quick Guide

📕 Book

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of the book. Publisher.

📰 Journal Article

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, volume(issue), pages.

🌐 Website

Author/Organization. (Year). Title of page. URL

📚 In-text Citation

Parenthetical: (Author, Year)

Narrative: Author (Year)

📝 Data Collection Methods

📋 Questionnaire

A structured set of questions used to collect information from respondents.

🎤 Interview

Direct conversation used to explore participants' experiences and views.

👥 Focus Group

Guided group discussion used to explore shared and different perspectives.

👀 Observation

Systematic observation of social behaviors or situations.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a research topic?

A research topic is the main subject or issue that a researcher intends to investigate.

What is a research problem?

A research problem is an issue, gap or concern that requires systematic investigation.

What is a research objective?

A research objective states what the study intends to achieve.

What is a research question?

A research question identifies what the study seeks to investigate or answer.

What is APA 7?

APA 7 is the seventh edition of the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association and provides academic writing and citation guidelines.

Can AI Mari replace a researcher?

No. AI Mari is a research support tool. Students should verify information, follow university requirements and use appropriate academic sources.
